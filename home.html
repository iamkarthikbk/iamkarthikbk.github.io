bio.

I am an engineer, deep sea diver, and a fitness enthusiast amongst many other things.

I am currently with InCore as a Senior Design Engineer. It has been about 5 years. I am also a research scholar with the Integrated Circuits and Systems group at IIT Madras. Prior to this, I was with the Centre for Heterogeneous and Intelligent Processing Systems at PES. Prior to that, I was a mentee at the Linux Foundation.

My research interests lie in Formal Methods for Logic Verification, Electronic Design Automation and Computer Architecture.
